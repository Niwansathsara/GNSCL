ABOUT GNS CLASS LIBRARY

A free, student-built space for learning and A/L preparation.

GNS Class Library is an independent student project created to help learners find, organise and revisit useful class lessons in one convenient place. The service is provided completely free of charge to support educational progress. The website organises lesson links and embedded resources; it does not claim to create or own third-party lessons.

YOUR A/L STUDY JOURNEY

Use these resources purposefully and work towards A/L success.

Videos are most useful when they become part of a steady study plan. Use the library to support your learning—not as a replacement for your teachers, textbooks or your own practice.

01
Plan by subject and topic

Choose a relevant playlist and set a realistic weekly target.

02
Learn actively

Pause when needed, make your own notes and revisit concepts you find difficult.

03
Practise what you learn

Combine each lesson with textbook exercises, past-paper questions and timed practice.

04
Stay consistent and ask for guidance

Review your progress regularly, follow your teachers' advice and keep moving forward step by step.

Small, consistent efforts can make a meaningful difference. Use the resources wisely, protect your study time and keep working towards your goals.
